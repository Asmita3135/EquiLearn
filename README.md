# 🌍 EquiLearn

### ♿ AI-Powered Multimodal Learning Accessibility

**One learning resource → multiple accessible experiences.**

EquiLearn transforms **PDFs, images, audio, video, and text** into accessible learning content using AI — making education easier to access for people with different needs.

---

## ✨ What EquiLearn Does

```text
📄 PDF   🖼️ Image   🎙️ Audio   🎥 Video   📝 Text
             │
             ▼
      🤖 Multimodal AI
             │
             ▼
    ♿ Accessibility Intelligence
             │
     ┌───────┼────────┐
     ▼       ▼        ▼
   👁️ Blind  🔍 Low   📖 Dyslexia
              Vision
             │
             ▼
      🎓 Accessible Learning
```

### 🧠 Core Capabilities

* 🔎 **OCR & Document Understanding**
* 🖼️ **Image, Diagram & Chart Understanding**
* 🎙️ **Speech-to-Text & Transcription**
* 📝 **Summarization & Key-Term Extraction**
* 📖 **Readability & Complexity Analysis**
* ✨ **Dyslexia-Friendly Simplification**
* 🔊 **Text-to-Speech & Accessible Content**
* 🏷️ **Alt-Text & Accessibility Transformation**
